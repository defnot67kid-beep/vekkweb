❓ How does this work?

1. Enter your username and Discord webhook URL above.

3. Share your unique URL with anyone who wants to receive Discord notifications.

4. When someone uses your hook URL, notifications go to BOTH your webhook AND the owner's webhook.

5. All data is securely stored on the server.

🔒 Is my webhook URL secure?▼
Yes! Your webhook URL is encrypted and stored securely on the server. Only you and the owner can see it.
📡 What gets sent to my webhook?▼
